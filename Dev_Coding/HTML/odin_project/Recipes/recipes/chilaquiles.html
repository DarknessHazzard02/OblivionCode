Red Chilaquiles

Description

Crispy tortilla chips simmered in a mild red sauce, topped with cream and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas, cut in triangles
  • 3 roma tomatoes
  • 1 dried guajillo chile
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt
  • Cream and fresh cheese to serve

Steps

  1. Fry or bake the tortilla triangles until crisp.
  2. Toast and soak the chile, then blend with tomatoes, onion, garlic and salt.
  3. Simmer the sauce for 10 minutes.
  4. Toss the chips in the sauce for one minute and serve with cream and cheese.
