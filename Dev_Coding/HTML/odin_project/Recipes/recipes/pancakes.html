Oat Pancakes

Description

Fluffy pancakes made with blended oats.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1 banana
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of cinnamon

Steps

  1. Blend every ingredient until smooth.
  2. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
  3. Cook small rounds on a hot greased pan, about 2 minutes per side.
