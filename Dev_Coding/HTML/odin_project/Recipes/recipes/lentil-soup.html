Lentil Soup

Description

A warm, filling soup that is ready in 40 minutes.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup lentils, rinsed
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 celery stick, diced
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 5 cups water or stock
  • Salt, pepper and cumin

Steps

  1. Sauté onion, carrot and celery until soft.
  2. Add garlic and spices for one minute.
  3. Add lentils and water
  4. simmer for 30 minutes.
  5. Season and serve.
