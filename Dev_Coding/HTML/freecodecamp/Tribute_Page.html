Dr. Norman Borlaug

The man who saved a billion lives

Here's a time line of Dr. Borlaug's life:

  • 1914 - Born in Cresco, Iowa
  • 1933 - Leaves his family's farm to attend the University of Minnesota, studying forestry
  • 1942 - Receives a Ph.D. in Plant Pathology and Genetics
  • 1944 - Rejects a 100% salary increase from Dupont to lead the cooperative Mexican Agricultural Program in Mexico
  • 1953 - Crosses strains of Mexican wheat with high-yield Japanese dwarf varieties, creating high-yield, disease-resistant dwarf wheat
  • 1970 - Awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for his work helping to avert global famine
  • 2009 - Dies at the age of 95

"Borlaug's life and achievement are testimony to the far-reaching contribution that one man's towering intellect, persistence and scientific vision can make to human peace and progress."

-- Indian Prime Minister Manmohan Singh
